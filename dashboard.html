NEW TO WORDFORGE?

How to Play

A little guide to get you started.

The Rules

  • 1 Find the hidden word. Guess a five-letter word to uncover the answer.
  • 2 You get six attempts. Each guess must contain five letters.
  • 3 Watch the tile colours. The colours give you clues after each guess.
  • 4 Use the clues wisely. Adjust your next guess until you find the word.

What Do the Colours Mean?

W
Green — Perfect Match

The letter is in the word and in the correct position.

O
Yellow — Wrong Position

The letter is in the word, but belongs in a different position.

R
Gray — Not in the Word

The letter is not in the word, or no unused occurrence remains.

Quick Example

Imagine the hidden word is PLANT and your guess is CRANE.

A and N are green because they are in the correct positions. C, R and E are gray because they are not in the hidden word.

Controls

ENTER Submit your guess
BACKSPACE Remove the last letter

You can use either your physical keyboard or the on-screen keyboard.
